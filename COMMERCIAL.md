# Commercial licensing

UIForge is public source and free for noncommercial use.

You may read the code, fork the repository, contribute pull requests, learn from it, and use it for personal, hobby, educational, and other noncommercial purposes under the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE).

## When you need a commercial license

You need a paid commercial license from Superlapie if you use UIForge — including its editor, CLI, schema, compiler, runtime helpers, or generated scene pipeline — for a commercial purpose.

That generally includes:

- a commercial game or interactive product;
- a product or service sold to customers or clients;
- internal studio tooling used to ship commercial products;
- SaaS, hosted, or managed offerings built on or around UIForge;
- client work where the deliverable is a commercial product; or
- redistributing UIForge, or a modified fork, as part of a commercial offering.

## What stays free

Noncommercial use remains free, including personal learning, hobby projects, contributions, documentation, and qualifying noncommercial organizations under the PolyForm terms.

## How to get a commercial license

Open a [GitHub Discussion](https://github.com/Superlapie/UIForge/discussions) or an [Issue](https://github.com/Superlapie/UIForge/issues) titled `Commercial license inquiry` with your intended use, team size, and distribution model.
